## UI contract
These are shared screen groups. Implement the portions required by this story; related stories own the remaining behavior. All affected screens need loading, empty, validation, forbidden and conflict states and keyboard-accessible controls.

### UI-07 — Customer list
**Routes:** `/o/[org]/sales/customers`
**Access:** sales.read; contacts.write for editing
**Structure:** Searchable table: name, phone/email, open receivables, overdue, available credits, advances and status. Filters for active/overdue. New-customer drawer.
**Actions:** Create, edit, archive, import, open profile.
**Validation:** Credit limit nonnegative; contact names can duplicate with warning; financial history blocks deletion.
**Expected outcome:** A customer with an advance is not shown as having earned revenue from that advance.

### UI-09 — Vendor list
**Routes:** `/o/[org]/purchases/vendors`
**Access:** purchases.read
**Structure:** Table: vendor, contact, outstanding AP, overdue, available vendor credits, advances, status. Search and due-date filters.
**Actions:** Create/edit/archive vendor, import, open profile.
**Validation:** No hard deletion of used vendor; isolate purchase data from sales-only users.
**Expected outcome:** List totals bridge to AP aging and do not include customer-side balances.

### UI-12 — Invoice list
**Routes:** `/o/[org]/sales/invoices`
**Access:** sales.read
**Structure:** Tabs All/Drafts/Awaiting approval/Posted; columns number, customer, issue/due dates, total, residual, settlement badge, delivery badge, overdue marker.
**Actions:** Create, filter, open, duplicate into new draft, export authorized list.
**Validation:** Do not put Paid, Sent and Posted in one mutually exclusive state column. No bulk silent posting/deletion.
**Expected outcome:** A posted invoice with failed email remains in posted revenue and separately shows delivery failed.
