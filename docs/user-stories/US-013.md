## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Use canonical decimal strings and exact arithmetic with documented half-up and line-total rules.
- [ ] Support six-decimal quantity/unit price and two-decimal monetary storage without JavaScript float authority.
- [ ] Validate negative/zero rules, excess scale, NaN, infinity and overflow at boundaries.
- [ ] Golden inclusive/exclusive illustrative tax and fractional quantity cases agree across service and database.
