## UI contract
These are shared screen groups. Implement the portions required by this story; related stories own the remaining behavior. All affected screens need loading, empty, validation, forbidden and conflict states and keyboard-accessible controls.

### UI-16 — Customer credit notes
**Routes:** `/o/[org]/sales/credits; /sales/credits/new; /sales/credits/[id]`
**Access:** sales.read/write
**Structure:** Original invoice lookup, eligible lines, original tax snapshot, reason, proposed amounts and intended application; list/detail views.
**Actions:** Create partial/full credit, submit/post, apply or leave available, link to refund.
**Validation:** Cumulative credits cannot exceed eligible original basis without a separately reviewed adjustment. Account choice respects earned/deferred recognition.
**Expected outcome:** Partial credit preserves the remaining invoice and produces the exact appropriate AR/tax reduction.

## Database/accounting guards
- [ ] [DB-G06](https://github.com/wdmahbubur/accounts-management-solution/blob/master/docs/03-database-design.md) **Complete subledger:** Each control ledger line has one matching open item; other ledger lines have none; same party/account/amount/side.
- [ ] [DB-G07](https://github.com/wdmahbubur/accounts-management-solution/blob/master/docs/03-database-design.md) **Allocation capacity:** Lock both items in ID order; same tenant/party/control/currency; dates not earlier than source availability; cumulative allocated amount stays within 0..original at every effective-date boundary, not just today.
- [ ] [DB-G09](https://github.com/wdmahbubur/accounts-management-solution/blob/master/docs/03-database-design.md) **Reversal integrity:** Source is posted and not already reversed; exact swapped amounts; correct reversal period; dependent settlements/reconciliation resolved.
- [ ] [DB-G10](https://github.com/wdmahbubur/accounts-management-solution/blob/master/docs/03-database-design.md) **Approval validity:** Expected source version/digest and policy snapshot must match; authorized approver; maker-checker; approval invalidated by material edits.
- [ ] [DB-G12](https://github.com/wdmahbubur/accounts-management-solution/blob/master/docs/03-database-design.md) **Tax/totals:** Recompute line/header totals, recognition mode, original credit-line references/caps, non-overlapping tax versions, snapshot/recovery policy and explicit rounding; never trust client-calculated totals.
