## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Classify cash-equivalent line movements using approved mapping and exclude noncash transactions.
- [ ] Eliminate internal cash-equivalent principal transfers and classify fees explicitly.
- [ ] Reconcile opening cash plus net movement to closing cash and flag missing classifications.
- [ ] Unclassified movement makes the report provisional and blocks finalization; golden fixture shows financing 100000 and operating 2000.

### UI-39 — Cash-flow management report
**Routes:** `/o/[org]/reports/cash-flow`
**Access:** reports.read
**Structure:** Range; opening cash equivalents; operating/investing/financing groups; internal transfer bridge; unclassified exceptions; closing cash reconciliation.
**Actions:** Inspect classification, export provisional, finalize when complete.
**Validation:** Unclassified cash blocks finalized label. Non-cash journals and opening migration mechanics are not current-period cash flows.
**Expected outcome:** Classified net flow reconciles opening to closing cash; capital contributions are not operating revenue.
