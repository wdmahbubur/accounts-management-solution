## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Customer advance debits bank and credits customer-advance liability.
- [ ] Supplier advance debits supplier-advance asset and credits bank.
- [ ] Create party/control open items and show unapplied advance balances separately.
- [ ] Require amount/date/account/evidence and do not treat advance cash as earned revenue or incurred expense.

## UI contract
These are shared screen groups. Implement the portions required by this story; related stories own the remaining behavior. All affected screens need loading, empty, validation, forbidden and conflict states and keyboard-accessible controls.

### UI-18 — Advance management and application
**Routes:** `/o/[org]/advances; /advances/new; /advances/[id]`
**Access:** Finance / dues.allocate; scoped statements for billing
**Structure:** Separate Customer advances and Supplier advances tabs; original, applied, refunded and remaining totals; apply drawer selects compatible invoices/bills.
**Actions:** Record advance, apply partially, refund unused balance, inspect reclassification.
**Validation:** No matching across different controls without generated reclassification; same party and BDT; applications require explicit finance authorization.
**Expected outcome:** An application creates no new bank cash movement and settles both the advance and AR/AP control pairs.
