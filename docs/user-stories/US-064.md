## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Submission stores source version, material digest, allocation targets and policy snapshot.
- [ ] Separate submit, approve and post permissions and reject maker self-approval unless explicit policy permits.
- [ ] Any material edit invalidates approval and requires resubmission.
- [ ] Posting rechecks current authorization, approved version/digest and live allocation capacity atomically.

### UI-30 — Approval inbox and review
**Routes:** `/o/[org]/approvals; /approvals/[id]`
**Access:** approvals.read/decide
**Structure:** Pending/approved/rejected tabs; request amount, creator and policy; full versioned document, evidence, journal preview and prior decisions.
**Actions:** Approve, reject with reason, open source.
**Validation:** No self approval in maker-checker; stale digest invalidates; server enforces eligible role and approval count.
**Expected outcome:** Changing the document after approval requires a new review and cannot reuse the old decision.
